# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README provides a comprehensive overview of OctoAcme's project management processes and serves as a central hub for navigating all official documentation. Whether you are a new team member, stakeholder, or contributor, these documents will help you understand how OctoAcme delivers successful projects through structured, iterative approaches.

## Process Summary

OctoAcme employs a customer-first, data-informed approach to project management that emphasizes iterative delivery, clear ownership, and psychological safety. Our project management framework is built on well-defined workflows that guide teams from initial idea validation through deployment and continuous improvement. Each phase is designed to ensure transparency, minimize risks, and deliver measurable customer value.

Our process encompasses several key areas: **Project Initiation** establishes the foundation with stakeholder engagement and go/no-go criteria; **Project Planning** defines scope, backlog, and dependencies; **Execution & Tracking** maintains team rhythm through daily standups, quality standards, and progress reporting; **Risk & Communication** manages proactive stakeholder updates and escalation protocols; and **Release & Deployment** ensures reliable delivery with pre-release checklists and rollback playbooks. Post-milestone **Retrospectives** capture learnings and drive continuous improvement across all projects.

The framework is supported by clearly defined **Roles & Personas** including Project Managers who coordinate delivery and manage risks, Product Managers who define outcomes and prioritize backlogs, Developers who implement features and maintain quality, and QA/Testing teams who validate acceptance criteria. Our communication strategies include weekly PM-PdM syncs, twice-weekly team standups, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed.

Quality assurance is embedded throughout our processes with comprehensive testing practices including unit tests, integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests, and security scanning in CI. We track key metrics such as velocity, burndown, and success indicators defined in project charters, using dashboards to monitor errors, latency, and usage patterns. This holistic approach ensures OctoAcme consistently delivers reliable, high-impact projects while fostering a culture of learning and continuous improvement.
